The Constella principle
By design

Small print,
made big.

The numbers that matter are enforced by the contracts themselves, not by a promise on a website.

0+

Independent price sources every asset needs. If they disagree, that sleeve pauses.

0h

Minimum public notice before any change that adds risk. Cuts to risk apply at once.

0XLM

Gas you’ll ever pay. Fees, reserves and storage rent are on us.

0%

Most you’ll pay in fees a year. Earn nothing, pay nothing.
