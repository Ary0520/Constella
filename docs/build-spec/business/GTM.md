### Persona 1: Crypto & Stellar-Native Treasuries (Beachhead)
- **Who:** Founders, CFOs, and DAO council members of projects with $500k–$20M in treasury stablecoins.
- **Pain Point:** Keeping idle funds earning 4–5% yield while staying liquid for payroll (often in EUR/local currency) and taxes requires juggling 5 different dashboards, monitoring issuer health manually, and compiling complex tax-lot spreadsheets.
- **The Pitch:** *"One non-custodial token that automates your company's investment policy, balances dollar and euro obligations, maintains an instant liquidity floor, and provides audit-ready accounting."*

### Persona 2: Wallets, Fintechs & Anchors (Scale Distribution)
- **Who:** Consumer wallets (e.g., Lobstr, Beans, Decaf) and regional Stellar anchors.
- **Pain Point:** Users want passive yield, but building custom RWA integrations, regulatory filters, and rebalancing logic is too expensive and risky.
- **The Pitch:** *"Integrate one SDK. Offer your users institutional T-Bill and sovereign bond baskets under your brand. Split protocol revenue 70/30."*

### Persona 3: Lenders & Money Market Operators (Collateral Consumers)
- **Who:** Blend pool creators, Liqvid protocol, Lucent finance.
- **Pain Point:** Listing individual RWAs as collateral is difficult because each RWA has unique redemption horizons, oracle risks, and regulatory quirks.
- **The Pitch:** *"List one basket token instead of ten separate assets. Every share ships with a Collateral Passport: conservative multi-source NAV, verified instant liquidity, and standardized liquidation paths."*

### Persona 4: Asset Managers & Issuers (Supply Partners)
- **Who:** Spiko, Ondo, Franklin Templeton, Archax, Etherfuse.
- **Pain Point:** Need sticky institutional distribution and real utility for their tokens so buyers don't just redeem.
- **The Pitch:** *"We pack your assets into recurring institutional treasury allocations and make them borrowable across Stellar DeFi."*

## 3. The 10-Design-Partner Playbook (Phase 1 → Phase 2)

### Target List
Recruit **10 anchor design partners** from the Stellar ecosystem:
- 5 Stellar-native startup treasuries (projects funded in recent SCF rounds).
- 2 Ecosystem DAO treasuries.
- 2 Regional Anchor operators (LatAm / Europe).
- 1 Web3 investment fund.

### The Offer
- **Free Private Vault for 6 Months:** Zero SaaS fees ($3,000 value).
- **Custom Policy Blueprinting:** AI engine fine-tuned for their exact corporate runway and payroll requirements.
- **Dedicated Discord Support Channel:** Direct line to core engineering.
- **Co-Marketing & PR:** Joint case study published during Phase 3 launch.

### Partner Commitments
- Weekly 30-minute structured feedback call with product leads.
- Deploying a minimum of $25,000 to $100,000 into testnet and capped beta vaults.
- Written public quote and case study consent upon successful mainnet migration.
